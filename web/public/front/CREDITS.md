# Images in web/public/front

The four photographs below (three of them under "Who it is for" on the home page) are from the Eagle Peak search and rescue in
Yellowstone National Park, taken by the National Park Service (NPS / Jacob W. Frank) and published by
Yellowstone NPS on Flickr and Wikimedia Commons as **public domain** (works of the US federal government).
They show a real search; they are not Sightline's data. Resized to 960 px wide and saved as WebP; nothing
else was changed. No logos are shown.

## The home page's photo wall (`wall/`)

`wall/f0256.webp` … `wall/f0275.webp` are the 20 photos of the sample pack (`/sample/sightline-sample-photos.zip`),
from the HERIDAL search-and-rescue dataset: D. Božić-Štulić, Ž. Marušić, S. Gotovac, "Deep Learning Approach
in Aerial Imagery for Supporting Land Search and Rescue Missions", International Journal of Computer Vision
127, 1256–1278 (2019), http://ipsar.fesb.unist.hr/HERIDAL%20database.html, licensed
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Changes: cut and resized to 1000 × 750 for the
sample pack, then resized to 360 × 270 and saved as WebP. `wall/still.webp` (the still shown on phones) is
twelve of them in a 3 × 4 grid. The box drawn on `f0273` is the detector's 0.17 detection that the second
look confirms as two people (`web/src/command/__fixtures__/second_look/verdict.json`).

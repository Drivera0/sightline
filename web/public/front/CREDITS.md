# Images in web/public/front

The four photographs on the home page and in the About sheet are from the Eagle Peak search and rescue in
Yellowstone National Park, taken by the National Park Service (NPS / Jacob W. Frank) and published by
Yellowstone NPS on Flickr and Wikimedia Commons as **public domain** (works of the US federal government).
They show a real search; they are not Sightline's data. Resized to 960 px wide and saved as WebP; nothing
else was changed. No logos are shown.

Frank) |
